🌍 Explore Nigeria

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Explore Nigeria

The name Explore Nigeria was chosen because it clearly represents the purpose of the website, which is to introduce visitors to Nigeria's geography, rich cultural heritage, famous tourist attractions, traditional festivals, and travel destinations. The name is simple, memorable, and encourages users to discover the beauty and diversity of Nigeria.

Site Purpose

Explore Nigeria is an informational tourism website that provides visitors with reliable information about Nigeria's geography, cultural heritage, tourist attractions, festivals, and travel destinations.

The website will also include a contact page where visitors can submit questions or feedback. JavaScript features will allow visitors to filter destinations, display featured attractions, validate form input, and save favorite destinations using localStorage.

Visitor Scenarios

  • What are the most popular tourist attractions in Nigeria?
  • Which traditional festivals can visitors attend in different regions of Nigeria?
  • Which destinations are best for wildlife and nature lovers?
  • How can I send feedback or ask questions about a destination featured on the website?
