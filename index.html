About Me

My name is Ikechukwu, but my pen name is I.J. Boniface. I reside in Lagos, Nigeria, and am currently a Software Development student at BYU-Idaho, while also teaching Digital Technologies and English/Literature part-time at Divine Glory College, Lagos. In my free time, I enjoy writing poetry and plays, and watching gospel-themed films. My goal is to build a career at the intersection of software development and creative media, telling faith-driven stories through platforms like Nollywood. Nigeria

Nigeria is the most populous country in Africa, located in West Africa along the Gulf of Guinea. It is home to over 250 ethnic groups, including the Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa. The country's diverse geography ranges from tropical rainforests to savanna, and it's home to landmarks like Zuma Rock. Nigeria is celebrated worldwide for Nollywood, one of the largest film industries on the planet, and for its vibrant gospel and Afrobeat music scenes.
